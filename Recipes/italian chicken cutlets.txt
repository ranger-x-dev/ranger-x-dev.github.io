Recipe: Italian Chicken Cutlets

Ingredients:
1 package boneless chicken breasts
Olive oil
Eggs
Flour
Bread crumbs
1 jar of pesto
Roma tomatoes
Mozzarela cheese
Parmesan cheese
Seasonings: salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, italian seasoning

Directions:
1. Heat a frying pan with 1/2 inch oil until it sizzles when dropping a bit of flour in it. I like to use half olive oil/half vegetable oil.
2. Prepare bowls for breading: 1 for flour, 1 with a few eggs whisked, 1 with breadcrumbs. Season each with salt, pepper, garlic, onion powder
3. Pound chicken breasts to 1/4 in thickness. Season with salt & pepper.  Dip in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs. 
4. Fry the cutlet in oil for 3-5 minutes until all breading is golden brown, then flip. Fry on other side for 3-5 minutes.

5. Slice tomatoes and saute them in a few tbsp of olive oil.
6. Spread the cutlets out on a broiler sheet.
7. Spread 1-2 tbsp pesto over the top of each cutlet. Then spread the sauted tomatoes across each cutlet. Season with italian seasoning.
8. Top each cutlet with a nice layer of mozzarela and parmesan cheese. As much or as little as you like.
9. Place the cutlets in the oven on broil until the cheese is melted and starting to brown.

DONE! Serve with salad and garlic bread, or on top of your favorite pasta and sauce.
