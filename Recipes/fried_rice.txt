Recipe: Fried Rice

Ingredients:
Basmati Rice
Water
Eggs
Frozen Mixed Vegetables (Peas, Carrots, Corn, Green Beans)
Cooked Ham
White Onion
Sesame Oil
Vegetable Oil
Soy Sauce
Seasonings: salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, white pepper

Directions:
Preparing the rice:
1. Add 2 cups rice to pan. Rinse and drain.
2. Add 3.5 to 4 cups water to rice according to rice instructions.
3. Bring rice and water to boil. Boil for 1 minute.
4. Cover the rice.
5. Turn off the burner. 
6. Do not open the pot. Allow the rice to simmer, steam, and begin to cool without removing from the burner. Do not fluff the rice. 
7. Place the rice in the fridge overnight for best results.

Frying the rice:
5. Add a few tablespoons of oil to a pan, whisk 4 eggs, and fry them in the oil as one large flat omelette. 
6. Chop the onions, ham, and eggs into small pieces (pea sized). Chopping all ingedients to the same small size is important.
7. Add a few tablespoons of cooking oil, a few drops of sesame oil to the pan and fry a few large scoops of cooked rice. Fry the rice until all grains are coated in oil and separate.
8. Add onion, ham, and frozen vegetables to your liking and fry until vegetables are done.
9. Season with salt, pepper, white pepper, onion powder, garlic powder.
10. Pour a few tablespoons of soy sauce around the edge of the pan, allowing the sauce to reduce a bit, then incorporate into the fried rice.
11. Finally, add the chopped eggs and stir well.
12. Repeat these steps, frying the rice in small batches until done.
