Interpreting Heatmaps

When the loss oscillates or training is stuck, the heatmaps can give visual clues about what’s happening:

Visual Cue Likely Meaning
White dot on red Current loss is high in this slice; adjusting these two parameters could reduce loss.
White dot on steep gradient (colors change quickly) High sensitivity; network may overshoot minima if learning rate is too large.
White dot at a cross of red and blue Possible saddle point: one direction wants to decrease loss, another increases it.
White dot in a large flat blue area Plateau: network may move slowly; small steps may not change loss much.
Colors rapidly changing in multiple slices Jagged loss landscape; step-to-step oscillation is likely.

Tip: By comparing multiple slices, you can see which parameters are causing oscillation and which are near a local minimum.
